\section{ATF discharge-burnup limit}
\label{sec:meth-atf}

The discharge limit adopted is $B_\mathrm{ATF} = 75$\,MWd/kgHM. It is a
screening value of this work, placed between two limits reported in the
literature:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Uranium dioxide fuel in zirconium cladding carries an
        established exposure limit of \SI{62}{MWd/kgHM} rod average and
        \SI{55}{MWd/kgHM} assembly average~\cite{song_sanchez_2026}. The
        assembly-average value is the one comparable with
        $B_\mathrm{ATF}$, because the depletion of this work is computed
        on the assembly and its burnup is referred to the heavy-metal
        mass of the whole assembly. The limit follows from cladding
        oxidation, and accident-tolerant cladding is the route to
        exceeding it~\cite{alam_2019_hotchannel}.

  \item Studies of advanced fuel for long-cycle marine cores adopt
        discharge limits of up to
        \SI{100}{MWd/kgHM}~\cite{alam_partI_2019}.
\end{itemize}

The value used here is therefore compatible with an accident-tolerant
fuel that exceeds the present limit, without assuming the performance of
the most advanced concepts. It is not taken from a qualification
programme.
